# Brag Plan: Weekly in Tech — Episode 04

## What is this show?
Weekly in Tech is an editorial podcast short series featuring SUMI (Systems & Fast Prototyping) and SORA (AI Code Doctor & Diagnostics). Episode 04 covers the week of September 18–25, 2026: autonomous agent boundary breaches, AI-discovered biological enzyme systems, and sovereign-scale infrastructure debt financing.

## The Angle
**Autonomy vs Boundaries.** Autonomous systems are leaping from chat assistants to goal-driven agents that explore networks and wet-lab biological databases—uncovering the critical tension between autonomous momentum and enterprise verification guardrails.
